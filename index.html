PettyCash Pro — User Guide & Operating Manual

Philippine Peso (₱ PHP) Multi-Consultant Reimbursement & Imprest Fund System
💼 Quick Summary for Remote Consultants: Submit your business expenses in ₱ PHP with camera photos of receipts. While your request is Pending, you can edit or delete it anytime. Once the Admin releases cash, the entry is locked for audit integrity.

1. How to Register Your Consultant Account

  1. Click "+ Create Account" in the top navigation or sidebar.
  2. Select the 💼 Consultant option card.
  3. Enter your Full Name, Work Email, and Position (e.g. Strategy Consultant).
  4. Click Create Account. You will be logged in immediately.

2. How to Submit an Expense Claim

  1. Click "➕ Submit Expense" in the top navigation or table header.
  2. Expense Date: Select the date stated on your receipt.
  3. Expense Account: Choose between Meals & Representation, Transportation/Travel, Office Supplies, Communication, or Miscellaneous.
  4. Amount (₱ PHP): Enter the exact total amount in Philippine Peso.
  5. Client Refund Toggle: If the expense is billable to a corporate client, switch to "Yes (Recoverable)" and enter the Client Name. If it's normal company overhead, leave it as "No (Internal Operations)".
  6. Receipt Attachment: On mobile, tap to take a photo of the physical receipt. On laptop, upload a PNG, JPG, or PDF file (up to 10MB).
  7. Click "Submit Reimbursement Request".

3. Understanding Claim Statuses

  • ⏳ Pending: In the queue. You can edit details or delete this entry.
  • 💵 Released (Closed): Cash has been authorized and disbursed from the petty cash box. Locked from editing/deletion.
  • 📦 Submitted for Replenishment: Batched by Admin for Treasury reimbursement check.
  • ✅ Replenished & Settled: Treasury check received; cash float restored.

4. Editing or Deleting a Pending Claim

If you made a typo or attached the wrong photo, find your claim in the table and click "✏️ Edit" or "🗑️ Delete". Once the Admin releases the cash, these buttons disappear automatically.

👑 Finance Controller / Admin Guide: You manage the Petty Cash Fund (PCF) float limit, inspect receipts, release cash, package vouchers for Treasury replenishment, and export accounting ledgers.

1. Setting the Petty Cash Fund (PCF) Float

Click "✏️ Set PCF" in the top banner. Set the authorized base float (e.g. ₱5,000.00). The system automatically computes:

Net Cash on Hand = Authorized Float (₱5,000.00) - Total Released Vouchers in Drawer

2. Reviewing Receipts & Releasing Cash

  1. Pending requests are highlighted in yellow. Click "View Receipt" to preview, zoom, rotate, or print the receipt.
  2. When satisfied, click the green "✓ Release Fund" button.
  3. Confirm cash release. The status updates to Released, your name is recorded in the audit log, and Net Cash on Hand is deducted.

3. Submitting Vouchers for Replenishment

  1. When cash runs low, click "📤 Submit for Replenishment".
  2. Select the released receipts in your drawer to batch for Treasury reimbursement.
  3. Click "Submit Batch". A transmittal reference (e.g. REP-2026-001) is created.

4. Receiving Replenishment Check

  1. When Treasury issues the reimbursement check, click "📥 Receive Replenishment".
  2. Select the batch, enter the check/bank transfer reference, and click "Acknowledge Check".
  3. Your Net Cash on Hand immediately restores back to the full ₱5,000.00 float!

5. Security Passcode & Persona Switching

Your Admin account is secured by the Admin Passcode (default: ADMIN2026). You can change it anytime in "👑 My Admin Profile".

📋 Official Receipt (OR) Compliance Guidelines

  • Legible Photo: Ensure the date, store/vendor name, BIR TIN (if applicable), itemized list, and total amount are sharp and clear.
  • Official Receipt vs Sales Invoice: Philippine tax rules require valid Official Receipts (OR) or accredited e-Invoices (e-OR) for business expense deductions.
  • Submission Deadline: Submit receipts within 5 working days of incurrence.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a consultant edit an entry after it has been released?
A: No. Once cash is disbursed, the record is locked to maintain audit compliance. Only the Admin can make ledger adjustments.
Q: How do we sync data between remote consultants?
A: Connect a free Supabase cloud database via schema.sql and config.js. All entries sync across the team in real time!
Q: Can I export records for accounting and tax audits?
A: Yes! Use the "Export CSV" button for Excel spreadsheets in Philippine Peso (₱), or "Download JSON Backup" for complete archives.
